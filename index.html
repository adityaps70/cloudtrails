Who we are

Riders first.
Locals always.

We're based in the heart of Shillong and we grew up on these roads — the ones that fold through pine forest, drop into limestone valleys and disappear into cloud past Sohra. Cloud Trails started as three scooties parked outside a tea stall. Today we run a full fleet, still looked after by the same friends who used to ride along.

Rent for a day or a whole week and go where you like. Ask us at the counter which viewpoints clear up by 9am and which shortcuts dodge the traffic. Either way, the bike is clean, the tank is full, and we're a phone call away if the weather turns.

02

Good to know before you ride

Hill roads, real weather

Meghalaya's roads climb, drop and get slick fast when the mist rolls in. We brief every rider on route conditions, gear you up with a helmet and rain cover, and flag which roads to skip after dark. Carry a valid photo ID and driving licence — some routes near the border also need an Inner Line Permit, which we can help you sort.
